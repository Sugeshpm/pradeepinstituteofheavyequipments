What career prospects open up after I finish? +

Depending on your course, you can work as a JCB Operator, Crane Operator, Forklift Operator, Trailer Operator, or Hazardous Materials Driver — across construction, logistics, mining, and infrastructure projects in India and abroad.

Are your instructors certified and experienced? +

Every instructor is certified and brings decades of real-site experience. They share practical knowledge so you get genuine hands-on capability with every machine you train on.

What will I actually learn during the training? +

Hands-on operation of your chosen equipment, plus safety protocols, equipment maintenance, site assessment, regulatory compliance, load handling, troubleshooting, and documentation — everything an employer expects of a qualified operator.

Do you really offer 100% placement support? +

Yes. Our placement cell stays with you until you're hired — both for opportunities in India and overseas. We work with employers across the GCC, Singapore and beyond.
