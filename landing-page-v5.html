How do I choose the right course from your 11 programs? +

We offer programs across three tiers. Starter Programs are ideal if you want to specialise in a single machine — Forklift, Trailer, Crane, or Excavator. Multi-Skill Programs cover multiple machines and are great for warehouse, logistics, transport, or construction careers. Advanced International Diplomas are designed for maximum earning potential in India and abroad. Not sure? Call us and our counsellors will recommend the right fit based on your age and goals.

Is the placement support actually 100%? +

Yes. Our placement cell stays active with you after course completion — connecting you to employers in India and abroad. We also partner with pijobs.in, India's life-tech marketplace for jobs. We provide interview preparation and support until you land a confirmed position.

What qualifications do I need to join? +

For the 3-month program you need to be 18+ — completed 10th, +2, or any degree. For the 9-month program you should be 20+. No prior driving or machine experience is required — we train you from scratch on our professional training yard.

Are the licences and certificates government recognized? +

Absolutely. Pradeep's Institute has been Government-approved since 1965 and is ISO certified. We issue operator cards for 8 types of heavy equipment and process up to 10 government driving licences through official channels. Our certifications are recognized by employers across India and abroad.

How do I enrol and where is the institute? +

Call us at +91 94296 90402 or fill the form on this page. We are located opposite Rameswaram Temple, Karuvelipady, Thoppumpady, Ernakulam – 682005. Walk-ins welcome. Our counsellors will guide you through course selection and batch scheduling.
